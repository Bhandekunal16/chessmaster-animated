# Grandmaster Chess

## Features

- Play locally with two players on one device or against the built-in computer opponent.
- Choose Novice, Intermediate, or Master AI difficulty.
- Play a complete rules-aware game with legal-move validation, check, checkmate, stalemate, castling, en passant, pawn promotion, and draw detection.
- Use optional time controls: no timer, Bullet, Blitz, Rapid, Classical, and Rapid with increment.
- View the move history, undo or redo moves, restart a game, flip the board, and request an AI hint.
- Export the current game as PGN and copy it to the clipboard.
- Pick from eight board themes and ten piece sets, including classic, minimal, fantasy, neon, pixel-art, and 3D-styled options.
- Watch piece-specific move and capture animations with visual impact effects.
- Enable or disable sound, haptics, theme-synchronised effects, and animations; choose Cinematic, Normal, Rapid, or Instant animation speed.
- Keep game preferences between app launches.
